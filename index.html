01 · Lean Six Sigma

Redesigning request and work-order workflows

CSD Operations Team Leader · 2024–2026

Problem

Requests, work orders, and quotations passed through many hand-offs. It was hard to see where time was lost to waiting, rework, or missing information.

Approach

  • Mapped the current-state value stream, separating working time from waiting time
  • Used Pareto analysis to find the few reasons behind most returned quotations
  • Applied root cause analysis to critical issues and escalations

Result

  • Redesigned workflows based on the map and Pareto findings
  • Resolved more than 20 critical technical issues
  • Mitigated 3 major schedule overruns through early intervention
02 · Power BI

Operations dashboards that replaced manual reporting

CSD Operations Team Leader · 2024–2026

Problem

Management reports were compiled by hand in Excel every week, and workload and follow-up were hard to see in one place.

Approach

  • Defined the KPIs each reporting stream needed
  • Structured the source data so reports refresh without manual work
  • Built one dashboard per stream: requests, work orders, quotations, workload, performance, and management summary

Result

  • 6 Power BI dashboards used for management reporting
  • Approximately 8 hours a week of manual reporting replaced

For scale: the team handled approximately 9,500 customer service requests, 2,000 work orders, and 500 quotations per year.

Acoustic path analysis per AHRI 885

Each noise path is worked through by octave band: unit sound power, duct and elbow attenuation, end reflection, and room correction, giving the expected level in the space.

The gap between that level and the room criterion sets the insertion loss needed, so each sound attenuator is sized to its path rather than chosen generically.
