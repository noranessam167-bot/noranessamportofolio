CASE 01 · LEAN SIX SIGMA APPLIED TO HVAC OPERATIONS

Mapping and redesigning service-request and work-order workflows

  • CSD Operations Team Leader
  • Rabie Al-Takyeef Factory
  • 2024 – 2026
  1. Problem

    Customer service requests, work orders, and quotations moved through several hand-offs between sales, engineering, and data entry. It was hard to see where time went to waiting, rework, or requests returned for missing information.

  2. Method
    • Mapped the current-state value stream step by step, separating working time from waiting time
    • Ran Pareto analysis on the reasons quotations were returned, to focus on the few causes behind most returns
    • Used root cause analysis on critical technical issues and escalations
  3. Tool

    Value stream mapping, Pareto charts, root cause analysis, process flowcharts, Excel and Power BI for the data.

  4. Result
    • Redesigned service-request and work-order workflows based on the map and the Pareto findings
    • Resolved more than 20 critical technical issues
    • Mitigated 3 major schedule overruns through early intervention and resource reallocation

Pareto of quotation return reasons · quotation lifecycle · defect Pareto · value-added vs waiting time by step.

CASE 04 · AI AUTOMATION IN HVAC ESTIMATION

AI-assisted BOQ-to-quotation workflow

  • Senior Technical Office & Estimation Engineer
  • AL SAMMAN for Air Distribution
  • 2026 – Present
  1. Problem

    Bills of quantities arrive as Excel sheets, PDFs, Word files, and scanned documents, in Arabic and English. Each one had to be read and re-keyed by hand before a quotation could be prepared.

  2. Method
    • Split the job into stages: extract items, map them to products, build BOM and costing, prepare the quotation
    • Used AI for extraction and first-pass mapping
    • Kept an engineer review step before anything is priced or sent
  3. Tool

    AI-assisted document processing and workflow automation, connected to the BOM and costing steps.

  4. Result
    • A working, semi-automated workflow that reads Excel, PDF, Word, and scanned BOQs in Arabic and English
    • A consistent path from source document to quotation, with engineer review built in

ACOUSTICS

Acoustic Path Analysis per AHRI 885

Worked acoustic path analysis for a supply-air path (click to enlarge).

Method

Each noise path is worked through by octave band: unit sound power, attenuation from straight ducts and elbows, end reflection, outlet and room corrections. The result is the sound level expected in the space.

Attenuator selection

The gap between that level and the room criterion gives the insertion loss the path needs. The sound attenuator is then sized to that requirement instead of a generic one being applied everywhere.

Standards

  • AHRI 885 — estimating sound levels in occupied spaces
  • ASHRAE 70 — air outlet performance data used in the calculation
