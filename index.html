Real acoustic analysis — commercial HVAC noise study

Methodology — AHRI 885

AHRI 885 governs my acoustic analysis workflow. Each propagation path — supply, return, radiated, and structure-borne — is analyzed individually across octave bands (63 Hz – 8 kHz) before combining to RC/NC curve targets.

Sound Attenuator Selection Strategy

Rather than applying generic silencers blanket-wide, each noise path is analyzed and minimum insertion loss is determined — enabling a targeted, compliant attenuator specification that avoids over-engineering cost.

Standards Applied

  • AHRI 885 — occupied space sound estimation
  • ASHRAE 68.1 — measurement of sound attenuation in silencers
  • ASHRAE 70 — terminal air device acoustic performance
  • ASHRAE Handbook Applications Ch. 48 — Noise & Vibration
  • ISO 5135 — acoustical performance of terminal units
